Home / How It Works

From your cart back to you.

Here is exactly what happens between your click and your UPI, including what makes an order eligible and how approval works.

  1. 01

    Choose an offer

    Open Explore Offers and read the rate and conditions on the card. Conditions can exclude certain products, payment methods or coupon codes.

  2. 02

    Click through and shop

    Tap the button on the offer card. The store records that you arrived from Worthback. To keep that record intact:

    • Finish your order in the same visit and browser.
    • Pause ad blockers and don't visit other coupon or cashback sites before paying.
    • Use only the coupon codes the offer allows.
  3. 03

    Report your purchase

    Send us the store name, order ID, order date and amount through the contact page, within 30 days of ordering. Use the email address you'd like us to contact you on about this cashback.

  4. 04

    The store confirms

    The store reports your order to us as pending. After its return and cancellation window ends, it approves or declines the order. Cashback is approved only when the store approves.

  5. 05

    Withdraw to UPI

    When your approved cashback totals ₹100 or more, send a withdrawal request with your UPI ID. We check it against your approved orders and email you once the payment is sent.

Statuses

What each status means.

Reported

You've sent us your order details. We're waiting for the store's report.

Pending

The store has recorded your order. It is waiting out the return window.

Approved

The store has confirmed the order. The cashback counts towards withdrawal.

Declined

The store didn't confirm it, for example after a return or cancellation.

Paid

We've sent the cashback to your UPI ID.

Eligibility

Your order qualifies when…

  • you are 18 or older and live in India;
  • the purchase started from a Worthback offer button while the offer was live;
  • it meets every condition shown on the offer;
  • it is not cancelled, returned or refunded;
  • you reported it to us within 30 days of the order date.

Full details are in the Cashback Policy.

Questions

Good to know.

Is Worthback free?

Yes. Worthback costs nothing to use. You pay the store its normal price, and we never ask you to pay us anything.

Where does the cashback come from?

Stores pay Worthback a commission when someone buys through our link. We share part of that commission with you as cashback.

Why do I need to report my purchase?

Worthback does not use member accounts. When you send us your order details, we can match your purchase with the store's report and know who to pay.

How long does approval take?

It depends on the store. Most stores confirm an order only after their return and cancellation window ends, and some then take extra time to report. We email you when the status changes.

Why could my cashback be declined?

Common reasons are a returned or cancelled order, a coupon code the offer didn't allow, a purchase that didn't start from our link, or a product the offer excludes. See the Cashback Policy for the full list.

How do I withdraw?

Once your approved cashback reaches ₹100, send a withdrawal request with your UPI ID from the contact page. We pay by UPI only.

Will you ever ask for my UPI PIN or OTP?

Never. To pay you we only need your UPI ID. Anyone asking for your PIN, OTP, passwords or card details in our name is not us. Please report it to support@worthback.in.
