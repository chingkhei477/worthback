Home / About Worthback

A small reward, plainly done.

Worthback is an Indian cashback and rewards website. We help you earn a little back on shopping you were going to do anyway.

When a store pays us for sending you their way, part of that belongs to you. Worthback exists to hand it back, clearly and without fuss.

What we do

We list cashback offers from partner stores, each with its rate and conditions in full. When you shop through a Worthback link, the store pays us a commission. Once the store approves your order, we pay you your share by UPI.

How we make money

Our income is the commission stores pay us. We keep part of it to run Worthback and give the rest to you as cashback. You never pay us anything.

What we won't do

  • List an offer before its rate and conditions are confirmed.
  • Ask you for fees, your UPI PIN, OTPs, passwords or card details.
  • Sell your personal information.
