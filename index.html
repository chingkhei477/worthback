The fine print, in plain words

What decides your cashback.

Tracking

Your order is linked to Worthback only when you reach the store through our link and finish buying in that visit. Ad blockers, other coupon sites or switching apps can break the link.

Approval

Stores confirm orders after their return and cancellation window closes. Returned or cancelled orders don't earn cashback. Timing depends on each store.

Withdrawal

Approved cashback is paid by UPI only. You can request a withdrawal once your approved cashback adds up to ₹100 or more.
